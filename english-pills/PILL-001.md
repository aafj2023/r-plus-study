# 💊 English Pill #001

📌 **WORD OF THE DAY**

**Word:** sort out (phrasal verb)
**Pronunciation:** /sɔːrt aʊt/ — "sórt-áut"
**Grammar:** phrasal verb (separable: sort it out ✅)
**Level:** B2
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Extremely common in everyday and work English

---

💡 **SIMPLE MEANING**

"To fix a problem, organize something messy, or deal with a situation until it's resolved."

- Natives use it constantly at work and at home — it's the natural way to say "resolver" or "organizar".
- Nuance: it sounds practical and hands-on, like rolling up your sleeves.
- vs. **solve**: "solve" is for puzzles/equations; "sort out" is for real-life messes.
- vs. **fix**: "fix" is for broken things; "sort out" is for confusing or disorganized situations.

---

🗣 **REAL LIFE EXAMPLES**

1. "Don't worry about the schedule conflict — I'll sort it out with the manager today."
2. "I finally sorted out my inbox. It was a complete mess."

🇧🇷 **Tradução:** resolver / organizar / dar um jeito em

**Similar words:**
- fix up
- work out

**Difference:**
"Work out" = encontrar a solução pensando; "sort out" = agir e resolver de fato. "Fix up" = consertar/arrumar algo físico.

**Extra explanation:**
Nativos usam "get it sorted" (UK) como forma curta: "Leave it with me, I'll get it sorted."
