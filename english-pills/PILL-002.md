# 💊 English Pill #002

📌 **WORD OF THE DAY**

**Word:** come up with (phrasal verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kʌm ʌp wɪð/ — "câm-ÂP-uid"
**Grammar:** phrasal verb (inseparable: come up with an idea ✅)
**Level:** B2
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Extremely common at work and in daily conversation

---

💡 **SIMPLE MEANING**

"To think of and produce an idea, plan, solution, or answer — especially when you need one."

- Natives use it constantly in meetings, brainstorms and everyday problem-solving.
- Nuance: it highlights the *creative effort* of producing something from your own head.
- vs. **think of**: "think of" is just having the thought; "come up with" is delivering the result.
- vs. **invent**: "invent" is for products/devices; "come up with" works for ideas, excuses, names, plans.

---

🗣 **REAL LIFE EXAMPLES**

1. "We need to come up with a better name for this project by Friday."
2. "I don't know how he comes up with these excuses — they get more creative every time."

🔎 **HIDDEN TRANSLATION**

<details>

🇧🇷 **Tradução:** bolar / ter uma ideia / inventar (uma solução, plano, desculpa)

**Similar words:**
- think up
- dream up

**Difference:**
"Think up" é quase igual, mas menos comum. "Dream up" sugere algo mirabolante ou irreal. "Come up with" é o padrão neutro que serve em qualquer contexto.

**Extra explanation:**
Combina muito com: *come up with an idea / a solution / a plan / an excuse / a name*.
