# 💊 English Pill #003

📌 **WORD OF THE DAY**

**Word:** swamped (adjective / informal expression)
**Pronunciation:** /swɒmpt/ — "suÔmpt"
**Grammar:** adjective (be swamped / be swamped with something)
**Level:** B2
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very common in work conversations, emails and small talk

---

💡 **SIMPLE MEANING**

"Extremely busy — with so much work or so many tasks that you can barely handle them."

- Natives use it daily at work: it's the natural answer to "How's your week?" when you're overloaded.
- Nuance: paints the image of being *underwater* in tasks (a swamp = pântano). Sounds informal but professional-safe.
- vs. **busy**: "busy" is neutral; "swamped" is busy times ten.
- vs. **overwhelmed**: "overwhelmed" adds emotional stress; "swamped" is just volume of work.

---

🗣 **REAL LIFE EXAMPLES**

1. "Can we reschedule? I'm completely swamped this week."
2. "She's been swamped with emails since she got back from vacation."

🇧🇷 **Tradução:** atolado / soterrado de trabalho / na correria total

**Similar words:**
- snowed under
- slammed

**Difference:**
"Snowed under" (UK) e "slammed" (US, mais informal) são quase sinônimos. "Swamped" é o mais universal dos três — funciona em qualquer país e contexto.

**Extra explanation:**
Collocation-chave: *swamped WITH* — "swamped with work / emails / requests".
